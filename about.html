About Us

[Placeholder] A short introduction — who runs this ministry and why.

It all began during the Covid shutdown, when the churches were closed and we couldn't attend. After a few weekends spent at home, my wife and I started looking for something meaningful to do with our free time. We decided to give away Christian books, free of charge, to anyone who wanted them.

As we prayed about the idea, God led us to a lively spot along the Pacific Ocean. There we set up a table with some Bibles, a handful of Christian books, and a simple sign — and while we waited, we prayed that God would send to our table the very people who needed those books most. To our surprise, so many people stopped by, eager to receive them. We never expected such warmth.

After doing this for a while, we felt inspired to reach even more people, without being tied to a single spot. That's how the idea of this website was born — a place where anyone can find us and receive free Christian books, so that God's word may spread and more hearts may be saved.
